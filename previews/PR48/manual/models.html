Models and gradients ​

b-valueµs/µm² (= s/mm²)1000
Permeability, relaxivityµm/µs (= m/s)1e-5
Gradient amplitudeT/m
Gyromagnetic ratiorad / (µs µm T/m)2.67513e-4 for water protons

Model ​

A Model contains the mesh and the coefficients of each compartment (initial spin density rho, diffusion tensor D, relaxation time T2) and of each boundary (permeability or relaxivity kappa), and the gyromagnetic ratio gamma. coefficients distributes the coefficients of the layers and the ECS of a recipe over the compartments and boundaries:

julia
coeffs = coefficients(
    setup;
    D = (; cell = [0.002 * I(3), 0.0005 * I(3)], ecs = 0.002 * I(3)),
    T2 = (; cell = [Inf, Inf], ecs = Inf),
    rho = (; cell = [1.0, 1.0], ecs = 1.0),
    kappa = (; cell_interfaces = [1e-5], cell_boundaries = [0, 0], cell_ecs = 1e-5, ecs = 0),
    gamma = 2.67513e-4,
)
model = Model(; mesh, coeffs...)
matrices = assemble_matrices(model)

assemble_matrices assembles the finite element matrices, in parallel on all Julia threads (start Julia with --threads=auto). compartment_weights gives the contribution of each compartment to the signal without gradient.

Gradient sequences ​

A ScalarGradient has a fixed direction and a time profile:

  • PGSE: two pulses of duration delta, separated by Delta;

  • DoublePGSE: two PGSE sequences;

  • CosOGSE and SinOGSE: oscillating gradients.

The amplitude is given directly, or by the b-value:

julia
gradient = ScalarGradient([1.0, 0.0, 0.0], PGSE(5000.0, 20000.0); bvalue = 1000, gamma)
bvalue(gradient, gamma)

A GeneralGradient is any function of time, with a varying direction. Directions for high angular resolution imaging are given by unitcircle and unitsphere.
